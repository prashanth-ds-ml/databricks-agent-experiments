# candy_distributor: what is this data?

This data likely represents a candy distribution business, with the main tables probably meaning the following: `candy_factories` lists the company's manufacturing facilities, `candy_products` describes the various candies produced, and `candy_sales` tracks the sales of these products. The `candy_targets` table may contain sales targets for different divisions of candies. Two things worth investigating are how sales vary by region and division, and how the company's pricing strategy (unit_price in `candy_products`) affects gross profit in `candy_sales`.
